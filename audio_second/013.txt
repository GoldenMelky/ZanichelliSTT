Do you know what a school prom is?
It's a formal party organised at school to celebrate an important turning point in your school career.
It's usually held in June or July at the end of secondary school in Year 13.
After all the exams are over, some schools also have a prom at the end of Year 11.
Proms are big news in Britain these days, but the idea actually came from the United States of America.
British schools never used to have proms. In fact, most had a simple end-of-year disco.
A prom is so much more. It's a very formal occasion where everyone has to dress up.
It's such fun to see your classmates in long dresses or black tie outfits when you normally see them in school uniform.
It's big business nowadays.
Many young people get to the prom by rented limousine with a driver.
Others rent a bus or another vehicle.
Then, there's the cost of the event itself, the hairdressers and photographers.
It can cost families over £200.
My classmates and I had our prom last June.
The school organised the prom, but we all helped with the decorations, food and music.
It was fantastic!
We had a delicious sit-down meal in the school canteen, beautifully decorated with ribbons and balloons,
and then danced the night away in the school gym, transformed into a magical ballroom.
What a great way to celebrate the end of an important phase in your life!
Did I meet my Prince Charming?
To be honest, I wasn't looking for anyone special.
