Foundation, Unit 1, Teens' Thoughts on Adolescence.
Adolescence is an age of transition between childhood and adulthood,
when you are old enough to have some independence,
but not old enough to worry about responsibilities.
Here is what some teenagers have to say about it.
Olivia.
When I look back at my childhood, I think I lived an isolated life.
My parents were overprotective and were always worried about me,
thinking something bad could happen if they weren't around.
I didn't used to be as friendly and outgoing as I am today.
Life is so much fun now.
I have sleepovers with my best friends, spend hours shopping in department stores,
and experiment with the most absurd clothes and accessories.
Oscar.
Anyone who is an adult will remember their adolescence as the stage in life when you are constantly with friends.
School plays a huge part in this.
Of course, no one enjoys getting up early in the morning or doing homework in the afternoon.
But let's face it, school is where you connect with friends.
Thanks to their help, you get to know yourself and experience your first important life events,
like falling in love for the first time.
Leo.
You may think I'm selfish, but right now, I'm enjoying the privileges of adolescence.
I love my freedom.
Driving a car without worrying about paying for the petrol and insurance,
or getting a part-time job to earn money to spend on nights out with friends.
As a child, I used to envy my older sister who could do anything she wanted,
and now it's my turn to live the life.
Isla.
Teenage life is exciting, but frightening too.
At the moment, I'm making decisions about my future studies.
There are so many great opportunities for us,
compared to those our parents used to have,
but I'm also afraid of choosing a course I won't like.
What happens if I make a mistake?
Do you see what I mean?
I'm already facing the responsibility of making important choices in life.
