Is growing up in a village better for children than living in a city?
For sure there are both advantages and disadvantages for families in the countryside and in a busy city.
Think about the close contact with nature and the community life that people can experience when they live in a village.
But consider also the wider range of opportunities you can have in a city.
Children that grow up in a city can take part in a lot of different activities.
For example, there can be drawing classes, kid-friendly workshops at museums,
not to mention all the different sports they can choose to practice.
Giving an answer to the town versus city question may be impossible because different people will always have different opinions.
However, it's interesting to mention that some years ago an Irish TV programme carried out an experiment.
Two city families experiencing parenting difficulties agreed to send their teenage children to the countryside.
They wanted their children to be raised by the village.
This was also the name of the TV series.
The teens had to spend some time on a farm, trying something totally different from their usual life.
They had to milk cows, take care of horses, and do other daily farm chores.
They did all these activities collaborating together and with the people from the village.
During the whole programme, the teenagers were under the supervision of an experienced psychotherapist.
Was it successful?
There were a lot of viewers and they loved the programme,
especially because, in the end, the teenagers liked their new environment,
although it was only on a temporary basis.
It could be just a coincidence or the power of a close-knit community.
Anyway, the popular proverb,
it takes a village to raise a child,
proved to be true, at least for once.
