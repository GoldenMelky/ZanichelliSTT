Welcome to our podcast series, Small Changes for a Big Impact.
Do you know what your carbon footprint is? It's the total amount of greenhouse gases that are generated by your daily actions.
Every time you ride your scooter, turn on the lights at home, or eat processed food, you are leaving footprints on the environment and making them bigger.
Ready to join the eco-friendly club and become a Carbon Zero superhero?
Here are some tips on how to reduce your carbon footprint.
First of all, reduce waste by recycling paper, plastic, and glass.
Also, try to buy products with little packaging and say goodbye to single-use plastics.
Bring your own reusable bags, water bottles, and containers.
It's a small change that makes a big difference.
Secondly, power down.
Turn off lights and gadgets and unplug chargers when you're not using them.
It's a simple move that saves energy and reduces your carbon emissions.
Be a gadget guru!
Choose energy-efficient gadgets and appliances.
They're just as cool, but use less power so you save energy and money.
And why don't you reduce your screen time whenever possible?
Less time on gadgets means less energy consumed.
And it's a chance for you to explore the non-digital world.
Drive smart.
Share rides with friends or use public transport when going to places.
It's not just fun.
It's eco-friendly.
Eat sustainably.
Do you know that methane, which is a powerful greenhouse gas, is also produced by animals?
Reduce meat consumption.
Go for locally produced, organic, and seasonal foods.
It's a win for your health and the planet.
Fashionistas, listen up.
It's time for a fashion treasure hunt.
Check out second-hand stores.
It's a sustainable way to shop, and you might find unique stylish pieces while giving a second life to clothes.
You water superhero!
You'd better take shorter showers and turn off the tap when you're not using it.
Every drop saved helps save the planet.
Wanna be a nature ambassador?
Get involved in community clean-ups.
Join a tree planting project.
Or simply plant a tree in your back garden.

Also, consider becoming a paper saver.
Use both sides of the paper when taking notes or just doodling.
Every little space counts.
Lastly, be a green activist.
Spread the word, share these tips with friends, and encourage others to join the eco-friendly club.
Each small action contributes to a greener and more sustainable future.
