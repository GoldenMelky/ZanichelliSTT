Do people still read newspapers?
According to statistics, many people still read print newspapers every day.
But the majority get their news from digital news websites or apps.
Some of these are reliable, but others can contain fake news.
In a recent survey, over 50% of teens said they read their news on social media.
Many added that they didn't really follow current affairs.
I wondered how many of them checked their sources and whether they often clicked on clickbait headlines or ads.
