Why don't you try reflexology?
Reflexology has existed for about 5,000 years and is becoming an increasingly popular way to reduce pain and stress.
It is based on the principle that our hands, feet and ears are made up of zones which reflect the different organs, body parts and systems.
1. When you stimulate these areas with specific massage techniques, you can reduce pain in the related parts of the body and improve blood circulation.
This gives you a sense of well-being and more energy.
2. It can be also good for children and teens.
If you want to learn some of the basic techniques, all you need is some simple instructions and a reflexology map which shows the major reflex points.
In the foot map, the left foot corresponds to the left side of the body and all the organs you can find there.
3. For example, the liver is on the right side of the body and therefore the corresponding reflex area is on the right foot.
4. Reflexologists use thumb and finger techniques to apply pressure.
4. For instance, when time is limited and the person really needs to relax, the therapist will choose just to work on the ears.
5. They are not just massaging the foot when they do this, but stimulating corresponding parts of the body.
It is all about applying pressure in the right way.
Reflexology can be useful to treat a variety of symptoms, including headaches and stomach problems.
But it can also be useful if you are anxious or worried.
5. If I were you, I would try it.
