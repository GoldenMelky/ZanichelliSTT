Hi, and many thanks for inviting me. I've been an advertising agent for 10 years now.
I did a couple of other jobs after graduating, first as a radio presenter and then as a photographer for a magazine.
However, I had always wanted to work in advertising and when this job came up, my family told me to go for it.
Successful advertising uses a variety of techniques to influence the consumer.
It works on positive memories and emotions that affect our behaviour over time and persuade us to buy something at a later date.
The secret of marketing is that it has to reach the subconscious levels of the brain in order to work.
We instinctively look at something that someone else is looking at.
This is why ads often include a model looking at the main target or message.
I often spend a lot of time choosing the model for an ad.
I think it's best to use happy, smiling faces because we tend to imitate the expression of the person we are looking at.
I was once asked what I found particularly hard.
I replied that finding the right slogan was the hardest thing.
The secret is finding a catchy phrase that people can easily remember.
Another question I'm often asked is what the most exciting moments of my job are.
Perhaps the answer is meeting celebrities.
