This year, for the very first time, our school has organised a science and tech festival,
and we would be delighted if you all came to visit it.
It'll take place from the 16th to the 18th of February in the school assembly hall.
The fair will open at 9am and close at 4pm every day.
It's absolutely free and will host an incredible number of stalls.
Each stall will be organised by a group of students
who will present a tech device they've invented and made
using their scientific and technological skills.
Maybe you're dissatisfied with some of your domestic appliances
and dream of having your house cleaned properly.
Or perhaps you still haven't found a gadget that can make life easier.
Come and see what students have come up with.
It's said that most of the jobs that will be common in 2030 don't exist yet.
But I think we are all aware that students will need soft skills
such as teamwork, creativity and communication.
At the fair, you'll meet students who have developed those skills
and can encourage others to follow the same path.
We are also proud to say that 57% of the students involved in the fair are girls.
This definitely shows a shift in perspective about scientific subjects,
which girls have up to now often considered geeky or extremely difficult.
Finally, students and parents will be told about the possible choices to make
in terms of university courses
and what jobs will trend in the near future
thanks to experts on further education
and career management counsellors present at the Jobs for Future stall.
Don't miss this fantastic mind-opening event.
Come and join us.
