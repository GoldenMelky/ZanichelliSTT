Did you know that April is Stress Awareness Month?
Its aim is to encourage people to talk about stress and mental health in general.
The campaign encourages people to take a 30-day de-stressing challenge
with a focus on three areas – physical, mental and emotional well-being.
The idea is that if you stress less by following some simple but effective ideas,
your lifestyle will improve.
Let's start with some physical goals.
If you are feeling stress symptoms like tension in your legs or arms
or have frequent headaches, you should get regular exercise.
This will help increase your flexibility and strength.
And if you set yourself goals and did a sport or activity you really enjoyed with friends,
it would make the challenge easier.
If you are looking after your body, then you will probably become mentally fitter too,
because exercise reduces depression and anxiety.
But there are other ways too.
Firstly, you should get a good night's sleep.
And to encourage that, you'd better limit your screen time.
You ought to keep your study space tidy and maintain a daily keep-fit routine.
But remember that you should always leave one day free for total relaxation.
The last area, and a really important one, is the emotional one.
You ought to connect with friends and ask for help if you need it and do the same for others.
Studies say that if we helped others more, we would feel better ourselves.
When you are very tense, you should try the five senses grounding exercise, which is as follows.
Look around and find five things that you can see, four you can touch, three you can hear, two you can smell, and one you can taste.
When you feel anxious, this will help you focus on something different.
You should also keep a mood journal to keep track of your emotions.
And try not to stay angry, but forgive and forget.
Lastly, you should always plan something to look forward to.
And remember that sometimes it's okay not to be okay.
