Good afternoon, everybody, and welcome to our weekly program Teen Stories.
This week, we are talking about a brilliant young girl, Marseille Martin.
Marseille Martin is an actress, an African-American activist, and a film producer.
Some time ago, at the age of 14, she became Hollywood's youngest producer,
and now she's a leading figure in the media industry.
Her life itself could be the plot of a film.
She was born in Texas and started acting when she was only five, starring in a commercial.
According to her biography, a photographer told her father to show her photos to a talent agency,
and they immediately hired her for a TV advert.
This was only the beginning.
When she was 10, her family decided to move to Los Angeles so that she could work as an actress.
There, she got her first major role in the sitcom Black-ish, for which she received several awards and nominations.
Marseille Martin launched her own production company at the age of 14, with the help of her family.
Genius Productions, as it's called, develops unconventional stories and writes scripts that have already been sold to major companies.
Marseille Martin has got clear ideas about what she does and what she wants to do.
Last year, she gave an interview and said she hadn't seen many young Black girls in the industry,
so she felt the need to give voice to them and increase Black representation in film and television.
As she became an activist for diversity, Marseille also embraced many other social causes,
like female empowerment and the importance of supporting teens' mental health.
She produced a video for the Obama Foundation and worked with ex-First Lady Michelle Obama for an initiative called Get Her There,
whose aim is to provide education for young women and girls around the world.
She also took part in the Mandela Project, which is a documentary series about the legacy of Nelson Mandela,
an inspirational symbol of freedom in South Africa and all over the world.
