Core, Unit 4, Exercise 23
Last year in my school, we did a project about Earth Day.
I found out it was first celebrated in 1970
and nowadays takes place every year on the 22nd of April
in more than 190 countries.
I talked about this event with my parents and my brother.
They agreed that protecting our planet is extremely important
and that's how we started celebrating Earth Day together as a family.
What did we do?
We decided to use as little energy as possible on that day
so no mobile phones and electronic gadgets were allowed
and of course we didn't watch TV.
Tough, right?
At the end of the day, I realised it wasn't so difficult actually
because we went for a bike ride and then played cards after dinner.
Time flew!
I forgot to mention that we also switched the lights of the house off
and only used candles in the evening.
We were very proud of our green actions.
Now my parents are trying to reduce the use of energy in the house.
They bought solar panels some months ago
and are thinking about investing in eco-friendly things for the house
such as a new dishwasher.
