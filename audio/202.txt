Expansion. Unit 8. Smart ideas for eco-friendly houses.
Sustainable houses have become common in the last few years,
and architects have studied a lot of ways to make buildings eco-friendly,
considering two key elements, self-sufficiency and zero waste.
One example is the Swedish Greenhouse Villa.
It is self-sufficient because it has its own system for collecting rainwater,
as well as one to produce electricity and heating.
The company, Naturvilan, has designed it to be like a real greenhouse.
It is shaped like an A and is made of glass and wood,
and inside you can also grow fresh vegetables and herbs.
The Welcome to the Jungle House in Sydney, Australia, is another example of green design.
Its minimalist exterior hides many smart ideas.
When they built the roof, they created a garden and a fish pond on the top.
The water of the pond is used to irrigate the plants, so that there is no waste.
What's more, the architect's family lives there.
They cultivate their own fruit and vegetables on the terrace and roof.
Recently, lots of celebrities have also decided to choose eco-friendly solutions.
Actors Mila Kunis and Ashton Kutcher, for instance,
have converted their Beverly Hills house into a sustainable farm with solar panels.
They recycled the wood from an old factory to rebuild it.
The Hollywood star Leonardo DiCaprio is an environmentalist,
and he has chosen only furnishings made of organic materials for his flat in New York.
Moreover, his house features an energy-efficient toilet.
Also, actress Jessica Alba has become famous for her green choices.
She doesn't buy plastic objects, and prefers vintage furniture for her house.
