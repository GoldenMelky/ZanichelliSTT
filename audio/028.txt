I want to talk about my experience in a very unusual school.
I go to Summerhill.
This is a boarding school.
It offers an alternative education because all primary and secondary students, boys and girls, are responsible for their education.
We learn to think for ourselves and be independent.
It's a totally democratic community because we choose what we want to do.
In fact, lessons aren't compulsory.
There are weekly meetings where every pupil or teacher can give their opinion or vote on a decision.
We can plan our own time and make the rules.
The school day starts with breakfast at 8.30 and lessons begin at 9.
We have a break in the morning and lunch between 12.20 and 1.15pm.
Lessons end between 3 and 5pm.
But the big difference between us and other schools is that we can choose to go to lessons, play in the park or do other activities.
It's very free, but that's exactly why we need to have good self-regulation and time management skills.
It's our decision to learn and we take responsibility for our learning.
