Foundation, Unit 2, Competences for Life. Are you wearing sustainable fashion?
Famous fashion brands like Prada, Valentino, Nike, Adidas and many others are making clothes and shoes from recycled plastic, paper or fruit and vegetables.
Can you imagine a vegan handbag made from mushrooms or pineapple or sneakers made from grapes? Yes, it is really happening. Sustainable fashion cares for the environment.
The use of natural material like organic cotton, linen or re-nylon is becoming more and more important.
Some companies, for example, are using re-nylon that comes from plastic bottles from the oceans or Tencel, which is the cellulose from wood pulp.
These materials are endlessly recyclable.
When you are buying clothes, it is good to think, does it fit? Does it suit me? Is it expensive?
But you must also think, is it sustainable? Is the material bad for the environment?
And what about the people making the clothes? Are they getting the right money for their work?
It is good to be trendy and follow fashion, but it is also good to know you are helping to make a better world.
