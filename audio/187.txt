Foundation, Unit 3, Hashtag Zero Hunger
According to the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations, FAO,
hundreds of millions of people all over the world are hungry.
But why does this happen?
The main cause of hunger is poverty.
If you are poor, you can't buy much food or healthy food,
and without it, you can't work or study properly.
If you don't work or study, you don't have money to buy food.
It is like a dog chasing its own tail.
Recent events are making things more difficult.
Wars, for example, stop food production, because farmers have to escape and abandon their land.
Climate change can make the soil dry and impossible to cultivate.
Is there no solution to the problem of hunger?
A lot of youths around the world think there are ways to solve the problem of hunger.
Take Ethiopian Asamanek Asmalash.
He says that in his country, FAO is giving farmers seeds and is teaching them how to store food properly,
so that the sun, or insects, don't ruin it.
Also, FAO helps schools to give free meals to poor children.
Asamanek is happy because lots of his classmates are back at school now that they can eat regularly.
In Mexico, Elvis Hernandez and his classmates are learning how to have a healthy diet.
In his country, a lot of people suffer from obesity.
So now, schools are organising nutrition programmes, where students learn about healthy eating,
keep school gardens, and have a cafeteria which serves a lot of fruit and veg.
