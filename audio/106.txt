Expansion, Unit 7. Presentation and Practice 1. Teen Types
Don't you think adolescence is a crazy age? One-minute teens are reliable, independent,
rational beings, and the next they are nervous or angry, grunting one-syllable answers to
adults' anxious questions. It is a little like living adventurously. Teens have so many faces,
you never know which teen you are meeting today. This week, four teens tell us their experiences.
The Screamager
My cousin Tanya is a Screamager. Her life is full of drama. She often feels less confident
if she has a tiny pimple on her forehead. The fact that there are fewer t-shirts in her wardrobe,
because her favourite one is still in the laundry, can make her moodier and more emotional than usual.
Her grumbling reveals that she is angrier than you would ever imagine. Your only chance is to be as
cool as a cucumber with her. The Dreamager
I think my half-sister Zoe is a Dreamager. She seems to live in her own time zone. She is a night owl and tends to be a daydreamer.
