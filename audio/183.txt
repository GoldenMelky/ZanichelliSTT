Boho chic is the style people choose for festivals like Glastonbury and Coachella.
But this hippie-inspired trend is common in everyday life as well.
Think of singers and actors like Selena Gomez or the British comedian Russell Brand.
What are boho chic clothes for women like?
Long skirts, floral dresses, denim jeans and jackets.
Let's not forget boots, including rubber boots in every season.
Must-have accessories are large earrings and, of course, lots of bracelets.
As for men, bohemian outfits are casual and baggy,
while the most common accessories are hats.
But also necklaces made of natural materials like wood.
