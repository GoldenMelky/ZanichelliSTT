Greenland. A trip I will remember forever.
Travel blogger Lisa Parks describes her adventure holiday.
My travel adventure camping on the Greenlandic ice cap under the northern lights last summer
was an unexpected experience that will stay with me forever.
If you like adventure, you will have to book a trip to the world's largest island.
But if your idea of a great holiday involves five-star luxury hotels, this country won't do for you.
The towns in Greenland are not well connected by road.
In summer, the best way to get around is by boat.
But when the fjords are frozen in winter, you might decide to travel by dog sledge.
You will also be able to fly to the major towns and sites by plane or helicopter.
Even if you are only there for a short visit, your trip might well involve a dangerous walk on the ice sheet.
For instance, while I was photographing a glacier, I was escorted away from it because it was at risk of collapse.
In Greenland, they have one of the biggest ice sheets in the world.
And these are vital for the planet because they help keep it cooler.
But if this ice cap melts completely, global sea levels will rise by 7.2 metres.
We must do our best to save it.
There are many places around the world where you can see the northern lights.
But where else are you going to see them while standing on another amazing natural wonder?
If you want to see the climate crisis in action, Greenland is the place for you.
During my stay, there was one of the few times when it rained on the ice sheet.
On another day, I visited Russell Glacier.
And just as I started to photograph the ice, a huge piece cracked and fell into the water.
The other journalists with me stared in silence for a long time after it fell,
as we understood the enormity of what we had just seen.
