Foundation, Unit 3, Presentation and Practice 1. What about eating out?
A. Manchurian Corner
Manchurian cooking mixes Chinese dishes with some Indian flavours. Enjoy our fried chicken
bowls with mushrooms or aubergines. Sources include soy sauce and sweet and sour sauce. You
can also find some rice or noodle dishes. B. Sushi bar
In our sushi bar, you sit at a counter and get the food directly from the chef. Sushi rolls
can be wrapped in seaweed or topped with raw fish like salmon. Soups and creamy desserts
are also available. How much does eating here cost? There are lots of different sushis at
different prices. C. The House of Poke
Fall in love with our poke bowls, freshly prepared with high-quality ingredients.
How many combinations are there? Endless!
You can choose up to two bases: rice, fresh salad, cauliflower, and two proteins: salmon, tuna, roast beef, shrimps, chicken, or cheese. Then you can add pineapple, tomatoes, carrots, cucumbers, or chickpeas. Finally, choose your topping: crispy onions, coconut, or almonds.
D. Veggie vegan
How much meat do we serve?
There is no meat and no fish, and there aren't even any eggs.
Everything at this place is 100% vegan.
As a main course, try a sweet potato lentil burger or black bean burger.
Are there any traditional desserts?
Of course.
Try the delicious triple chocolate pudding.
None of our desserts have dairy products in them.
E. Bubble Island.
Try our bubble teas with black tea and milk served with delicious tapioca pearls.
When you order, you can decide to add some ice to your bubble tea, or no ice at all.
Our menu also includes muffins, biscuits and cheesecake.
Thank you.
