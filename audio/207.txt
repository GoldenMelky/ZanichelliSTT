Expansion B1 Preliminary Training 3 Listening Part 3
Hi everyone! My name is Liz. I'm 17 years old and science is my passion.
Today I want to talk to you about one of my role models, Gitanjali Rao, or Rao for short.
She is a young STEM researcher and started loving science when she was about 8.
Rao has always been a very cheerful and helpful girl and she tried her best to make people happy.
She decided to use science to make the whole community feel good and optimistic about the future and promote global development.
She thought that it was also an opportunity to inspire other girls to study STEM subjects as figures show that only 30% of women are in STEM.
In 2020, Rao worked on a project that I think is great.
She created an app called Kindly that can prevent cyberbullying.
It works in a simple but effective way.
It uses artificial intelligence technology to identify cyberbullying at an early stage by analysing words that teenagers type into their smartphones and recognising the offensive ones.
Once they know that the word they are using can be classified as bullying, they can edit it and choose one that is harmless.
At the beginning, Rao was pessimistic about the success of the project.
She thought teens wouldn't like an app that controlled them.
But they thought it was cool and helped them to reflect more on the language they were using.
Teenagers tend to be very moody and sometimes use language in an aggressive way without thinking about it.
She was so happy that they appreciated the aim of the project, which is to learn from mistakes and become more responsible.
