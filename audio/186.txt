Hi, I'm Max and this is my recipe for a yummy apple crumble.
It's simple and very tasty.
Here's the list of the ingredients you need.
Four apples.
One tablespoon of sugar.
For the apples.
Fifteen grams of butter.
For the apples.
One hundred and sixty grams of flour.
Six tablespoons of brown sugar.
One hundred grams of butter.
A pinch of salt.
A pinch of cinnamon.
And now this is what you do.
First, preheat the oven to one hundred and seventy degrees Celsius.
Then, peel the apples and cut them into cubes.
Add the sugar to the apples.
Melt the butter and pour it onto the apples.
Mix for a minute or two.
After that, put the flour, the brown sugar, a pinch of salt and the cinnamon in a bowl.
Add the butter and mix it with the other ingredients until the mixture becomes crumbly.
Butter a baking tin.
Place the apples in it and then add the crumbly mixture.
Bake for about thirty minutes and serve the crumble when it's still warm.
It's delicious with a little vanilla ice cream on top.
