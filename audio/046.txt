Foundation, Unit 3, Presentation and Practice 1, Exercise 7
My favourite meal is dinner because it's the last meal of the day
and all the family sits down to eat together and chat about our day.
We have dinner at around 6pm.
Me and my parents usually have a grilled steak or chicken breast with a jacket potato straight from the oven
and some salad or vegetables.
My brother likes spicy sausages and he usually has three with two fresh tomatoes.
My sister is a vegetarian and often has a bowl of veggie soup cooked with some type of cereal in it.
She likes it well seasoned and always adds some extra salt to it.
I usually drink a glass of cola while my parents drink two or three glasses of sparkling water
and usually have a cup of coffee at the end.
A tasty dinner is the best way to relax and end the day.
