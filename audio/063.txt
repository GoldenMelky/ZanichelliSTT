For you to reduce food waste, fight world hunger and help the environment. First of all, buy local food. Zero food mile food reduces the energy we need to transport food and refrigerate it and helps the local community.
Or buy fair trade food. This helps to create jobs for people living in poor areas of the world and to support their economy.
To reduce food waste, you can...
Plan your meals. Create a shopping list, including only the necessary amount of food you need.
By planning your meals for the week, you can reduce food waste, save money and check that you are following a balanced diet.
Check your fridge. Before you go to the store, see what you already have in your fridge.
Read the expiry date on food labels and reorganise your meals for the day to consume these foods first.
Get a doggy bag. When you go out for dinner and have leftovers, ask for a doggy bag.
You can eat the food the next day and save on gas.
Be a sustainable cook. Experiment with new recipes using only the ingredients you have at home.
Leave the skins on your veggies and just throw away the parts that you can't digest.
Keep a list of wasted food. This helps to make you aware of how much food you are wasting.
