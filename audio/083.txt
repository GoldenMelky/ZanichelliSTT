Core, Unit 5, Presentation and Practice 2. Who knows these sports?
Cricket. Cricket is England's national summer sport and is very popular around the world.
There are two teams of 11 players who score runs by hitting the ball and a formal game can last from one afternoon to a few days.
What does the player's outfit and equipment include?
It includes long white trousers, a white shirt and jumper, a helmet with a face mask, leg pads, gloves, a very hard ball and a bat.
Fun Fact
The longest cricket match lasted 12 days.
Squash.
Squash is an important part of New Zealand's sporting culture.
Players use oval rackets to strike a small rubber ball, bouncing it off designated areas of each wall very quickly.
People play it in an indoor four-walled court.
Paul Coll is New Zealand's squash superstar.
He took up this sport when he was a child and now plays so well that he won a gold medal in the latest edition of the Commonwealth Games.

Rugby.
Rugby is the national sport of Wales.
The Dragons are one of its four professional rugby teams.
Where do they get their name from?
It comes from the Red Dragon on the Welsh flag.
The aim of the game is to ground an oval ball behind the opponent's tryline in the in-goal area.
The two teams of 15 players can run quickly with the ball in their hands, kick the ball and pass it backwards.
It isn't as well known, but women's rugby is now growing fast.
Fun Fact
A pupil at rugby school in England accidentally invented this sport in 1823,
when he started to run hard with the ball in his hands during a football game.
Do you know the Commonwealth Games?
The Commonwealth Games are a multi-sport, international event that takes place every four years.
Which countries join in?
The 56 member states of the Commonwealth of Nations.
These countries were under the rule of the British government in the past.
