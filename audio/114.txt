Expansion, Unit 7. Competences for Life. Happiness from Helping Others.
1. Joshua Williams' story is not just your typical story of a generous teenager doing good in his local community.
It shows how brave individuals can encourage their peers to become changemakers.
What's even more surprising is that this all started with the little boy.
2. One Sunday, four-year-old Joshua Williams from Miami Beach was on his way to church with his mother when he saw a hungry homeless man in the street.
He felt extremely sorry for the man.
Soon after this, Joshua saw a Feed the Children commercial on TV.
This organisation distributes food to children and families in need all over the world.
Joshua decided to do something, and with a $20 donation from his grandma, he created a charity called Joshua's Heart Foundation.
3. There are now 60,000 young volunteers working for the not-for-profit Youth Run Foundation.
Their network gives hope and help to people in underprivileged communities.
The main aim is to end hunger and break the cycle of poverty.
One way they do this is to provide boxes of basic food and personal items to families every month.
Joshua is now in his early 20s and is one of the best-known young motivational speakers in the USA.
During his travels, he always tries to make young people more aware of the problems connected to poor nutrition and how they can help others.
This way, he has the chance to collaborate with well-known food brands and earn money for his foundation.
5. In 2012, Joshua became the youngest recipient of the Champions of Change Award from the White House for improving food security in the USA.
His organisation started as a passion because making a difference and putting a smile on other faces made him happy.
When you give, you receive back.
And giving makes you feel good, he said in an interview.
And now, Joshua continues to look for new strategies to inspire young people to help their communities and people in need.
