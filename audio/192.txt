Core, Unit 4, Celebrating Sustainably.
Last month, I organised a birthday party for my cousin Violet. She turned 30, and like me, she is passionate about this cause.
First, I chose the venue. I decided the park near our house was the best option, because it has a covered picnic area.
As for the decorations, my aunt helped me. She put beautiful leaves and pine cones on the tables, and she also hung paper flags, instead of plastic balloons, all over the place.
I also bought tablecloths, plates, and napkins made from recycled materials. My brother and my mum gave me a hand with the food.
I chose local products, of course, and I prepared a lot of plant-based dishes and snacks. They are environmentally friendly.
My mother made a carrot cake with vanilla frosting. It was delicious.
When it got dark, we lit candles on the tables and kept chatting until late.
My cousin was really excited about her green party, and the guests were impressed too.
