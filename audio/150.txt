I'm going to give you some tips about becoming a more responsible tourist.
First of all, think local.
There are probably lots of places nearby that you haven't visited yet,
and new restaurants or hotels to go to.
These won't cost a lot to get to, and you'll find out more about your surroundings.
And if you're going further away, always try to support local businesses.
Are you going to use the car, or are you going to take public transport or use your bike?
The second tip is to consider trains and bikes, or even try a walking holiday.
It will be good for the planet and great for your health.
The third tip is to stay in eco-friendly accommodation.
In the future, there will be even more eco-friendly places to stay in,
from tents to yurts, cabins and tree houses.
We still want to travel to exotic destinations like Africa or Costa Rica,
and this means we'll probably fly there,
but we can at least go for eco-lodges and other accommodation that doesn't damage the environment.
The next tip is to learn to think ecologically.
When you travel, use a refillable water bottle, bring solid shampoo bars,
and don't overuse plastic.
You'll find that most hotels will let you reuse towels too.
And this brings me to the last tip.
Be sure you'll leave nothing behind you, or, as they say, leave only footprints.
This means you'll think carefully about what you do with any rubbish or waste.
When camping, for example, you'll show respect for both wildlife and other visitors by removing your waste.
And never leave a campfire burning.
Follow my tips, and you'll be a responsible tourist.
Have fun!
