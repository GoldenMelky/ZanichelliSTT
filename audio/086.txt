Core, Unit 5, Competences for Life, The True Athlete Project.
We all know what a positive difference sport can make in our lives.
However, we also know that sport can cause bullying, suffering and depression.
The True Athlete Project, TAP, is a programme that uses mindfulness, mental health training and mentoring
to create a more compassionate culture in sport.
It wants to encourage the idea of an athlete as someone who develops their mind and body
to help others and make the world a better place.
How does it work in practice?
For example, it matches Olympic, Paralympic or other successful athletes
with young, committed athletes for a year-long experience.
Young diver Milo French and Olympic swimmer Chris Cook.
16-year-old Milo's several injuries left him feeling frustrated and unable to train.
The pairing with Chris helped him greatly.
Chris changed Milo's outlook on sport and everyday life.
Milo said,
I found new ways to keep motivated when I'm feeling everything is going wrong.
Chris gave him some really good tips so he could keep his mind positive during his performance.
Milo also improved his ability to stay focused and relaxed.
Olympic diver, coach and mental health expert, Leon Taylor, joined the programme.
He is currently supporting a young basketball player from the USA, Colin Clarity.
What he says he enjoys most is to be able to share my knowledge and experiences
with these rising stars of the future and help them on their journey.
