When I met my wife Bethany at high school, I soon realized that the expression extended family here in the USA means something totally different from my Indian idea of extended.
In India, the traditional extended family includes relatives all living in the same household or in the same block.
Apart from being a tradition, there are a lot of benefits in an extended family system.
We all take care of and support one another.
Grandparents look after their grandparents.
And when an older family member needs health care, the family is the perfect alternative to an old people's home or hospital.
But the best part is when it comes to celebrating family events.
When my sister got married in India, we organized a colorful, one-week-long wedding party with hundreds of relatives and guests.
