Foundation, Unit 1. Competences for life. What is it like in a boarding school?
Education in Britain is compulsory from the ages of 5 to 16. State schools are free, but there are many selective independent schools. Some are single-sex, while others are co-educational.
Public schools are prestigious independent schools, and many of these are boarding schools, where students live and study during the school year.
The most famous are Eton, Harrow and Winchester. Twenty British Prime Ministers are Etonians.
On a typical day at Eton, boarders wake up at 7.30am, have breakfast and go to chapel, before having five classes during the day.
Dinner is at 7.40 and lights out at 10pm.
One of the key features of boarding school life is the variety of experiences.
There is an emphasis on sports, but also activities like dancing and drama, public speaking and photography.
Time management is an essential skill that boarders need to learn as part of their education.
