Hi everyone and welcome once again to our weekly programme about social issues,
helping others. Tonight's episode is dedicated to an organisation that helps and supports
homeless young people. We're going to hear from some of them this evening. Let's start with Mia.
I've always had a difficult relationship with my family and I often had to sleep on friends' sofas.
When I turned 16 I decided to leave home. At first I stayed with a friend but this put too much stress
on the relationship. Then a social worker told me about this charity and they offered me a room in
sheltered accommodation. When I first moved in I was very nervous but now I feel happier and more
confident and I've started to make friends. It's wonderful to feel that I've found a safe place
and for the first time I've begun to have hope for a better future. I'd like to go to college and my
greatest ambition is to become a counsellor for children living in difficult family environments.
Thanks Mia for telling us your experience. Now it's Zoran's turn.
Hi everybody. I was just nine when I arrived in Britain on my own as a refugee from Iran. I stayed in a temporary
centre for nearly three weeks and then I went to stay in different shelters. I was very frightened and
didn't even know which country I was in. Then I met a representative of this charity and he placed me
with a host lady in the town of Reading. My life has completely changed. My host has taught me English
and how to prepare lunch. Cooking has become my passion and I've begun to believe in myself again.
In fact, my big ambition is to open a restaurant one day and to help poorer people and those experiencing homelessness. It's just a dream, I know, but sometimes dreams come true.
