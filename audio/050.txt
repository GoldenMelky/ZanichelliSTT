Delicious Easy Brownies
Welcome to our kitchen!
Do you have little free time to read recipes, but a lot of enthusiasm?
Today you can learn a few tips on how to make great brownies from start to finish.
It's a piece of cake. You don't need much time or many ingredients.
Ingredients
10 tablespoons of unsalted butter
1 cup of sugar
1 cup of cocoa powder
1 teaspoon of vanilla extract
2 cold eggs
1 half a cup of flour
Salt
Procedure
First, preheat the oven to 180 degrees Celsius. Then, line a square baking tin with baking paper.
Place the butter with the sugar, cocoa powder and a little salt into a large bowl.
We generally microwave it for a few minutes to melt the butter. After 5 to 10 minutes, when the mixture is cool enough, add the vanilla and the two eggs one at a time. Then mix vigorously. Next, stir in the flour and continue to combine the ingredients, but not too much, as the brownies can become too hard.
Finally, pour the butter into the pan and bake the brownies for 20 minutes or until the sides are soft. Leave your brownies out of the oven to cool for a few minutes and then use a sharp knife to cut them into squares. Enjoy!
