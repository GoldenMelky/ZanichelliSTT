Foundation, Unit 3, Competences for Life, Cooking Tips from Social Media
Do you think it is time to learn how to cook? The world of social media can help you.
In a few minutes, or even in just 60 seconds, you can watch and learn how to create delicious-looking and tasty meals.
Are you looking for a new cultural cuisine? Are you interested only in vegan food? No worries! You can find lots of cooking tutorials on TikTok, Instagram, Twitter and Facebook.
For the sisters Mary and Ross, 15 and 13, cooking from TikTok is part of daily life.
Mary likes using the app to bake cakes, while Ross often cooks dishes like steak and chips with it.
Cooking is becoming cool, says Ross, because when your parents ask you to cook supper, at first you think it's too hard.
But when you're watching a pleasant video, it doesn't seem so bad, and you can also learn some new skills.
The other side of the coin is that social media can also negatively affect the way we eat.
The videos we see on social media can encourage us to eat unhealthy food because we are attracted by them.
Some fast food producers take advantage of this to promote their products.
