My sleepy town had a shock last week.
There was a sudden crime wave.
There were two murders, a bank robbery, a kidnapping and three arrests for drug dealing.
If you consider that the worst crimes the week before had been shoplifting, a mugging and an arrest for pickpocketing, you can understand the total shock of my neighbours.
Then there was the explanation.
The local film company had invented all these crimes as part of a TV series about the criminal world.
They showed a burglary and theft where two burglars broke into a house and got away with jewels and cash.
The actors then became vandals damaging parked cars and doing other acts of vandalism.
The final scene was supposed to be a hijacking.
They had organised a local bus and two actors dressed as hijackers, but then decided it wasn’t realistic.
In fact they decided to move to blackmail instead with the local mayor being blackmailed about his secret past.
When we all realised it was just a film, we relaxed and enjoyed watching our town under attack from the criminal world.
