Andrew and Joanne are going shopping.
Andrew has to buy a present for his granny.
He has asked Joanne to help him choose something, but he hasn’t got a lot of money in his wallet and can’t afford to spend much.
Joanne offers to lend him some money, but Andrew doesn’t like borrowing money from anyone and doesn’t want to owe any money.
He’s been saving for a while and decides to take some money out of the ATM machine.
He earns some money from dog sitting.
He doesn’t charge a lot, but this means he has some money to spend after paying for his bus pass and other essentials.
Andrew and Joanne look at the flower stall and the cake shop, but Andrew doesn’t want to waste money on something that won’t last.
In the end they buy a pretty vase.
It was a bargain and Andrew donates the change to a local charity.
He’s very happy and very grateful to Joanne for her help.
Then they go home to do their homework.
