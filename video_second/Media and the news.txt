How do you hear the news? How do you find out about what’s going on in the world? Do you maybe listen to the news broadcast on the radio or transmitted on the television? Or are you part of the generation who have turned their backs on traditional sources of information and prefer social media and podcasts? Do you rely on friends to tell you the latest breaking news or are you not interested in current affairs? The press has certainly changed.
People used to read newspapers and magazines to find out what was what in the world.
Nowadays newspapers are online and people rarely buy them.
Some people look at the headlines and maybe choose an interesting interview with a politician or a celebrity, or perhaps read a stimulating report about a current issue like climate change.
One of the problems with social media as a source of news is that it’s difficult to distinguish real news from fake news.
If someone told you some scandal about a person in the news, would you spread it immediately on your social media account or would you try to find out whether the source was reliable?
