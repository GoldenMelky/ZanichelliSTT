My class wants to organise a publicity campaign about making our neighbourhood greener.
We have begun discussing what to do and how to make people aware of our green goals.
It has to be like a PSA, a public service announcement campaign persuading people of the benefits of having more trees, more green spaces, fewer cars and more cycle lanes.
We thought of a huge billboard to put outside the school, but also flyers and leaflets in the local shops to promote the idea.
We need to launch the campaign with banner ads on the school website.
We won’t use commercials, because commercials are usually broadcast on television or radio.
We should persuade as many teachers and students as possible to help advertise our goals by talking to everyone they know.
You never know, if the idea takes off, it may go viral like a successful brand campaign.
We think visual advertisements influence people more than written ones, so join us by sending photos or pictures of places that need improvement.
Send in any new ideas and tell us what you think of the campaign.
