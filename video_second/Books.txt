Do you like reading? Andrew does, he always has his nose in a book.
Here he’s reading an adventure story about an expedition to the South Pole.
It’s a kind of biography about the expedition leader Ernest Shackleton and how he brought his whole team to safety against all odds.
Andrew doesn’t like novels – whether they are historical novels about the past or fantasy novels – and above all he hates love stories and romantic novels.
But there’s one exception; he likes sci-fi novels, especially about space travel.
His friend Joanne prefers films to books, but recently she’s started reading graphic novels.
She says they remind her of the wonderful fairy tales she used to read as a child.
She also enjoys crime stories with interesting plot twists and clever detectives, but she hates thrillers or horror stories.
Too scary! Recently she was given a self-help book called <i>Be a Better You</i>, but she hasn’t started reading it yet.
She says she likes herself as she is.
She can’t imagine herself as a novelist or poet, but she would like to be the protagonist in a romantic novel with a happy ending.
