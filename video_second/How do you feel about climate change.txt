Hi there! Today we want to talk about a very serious issue, climate change, and the huge impact it has on our lives: on the water we drink, the food we eat, and the air we breathe.
When I think of environmental issues like the greenhouse effect, pollution, deforestation, endangered animals or waste disposal, I feel overwhelmed.
There is a sense of fear that something awful is happening and not enough is being done about it.
I feel as if our future is not guaranteed.
Global warming is a fact and the world is getting dramatically warmer.
Going out on a summer’s day used to be a pleasure, but now it’s not because the heat of the sun is often just too much.
It’s hot, yes, but painfully hot.
The issue of climate change? It makes me upset, anxious and angry all at the same time.
In the area where I live, we are constantly experiencing extreme weather.
I do think we should let policymakers and leaders know what it’s like to be a young person living on this planet right now, and we feel like we’re carrying the weight of the world on our shoulders.
We know there is no planet B, and that we only have a few decades to radically transform our lifestyles so that everything is recyclable, reusable and renewable.
I long for a future world that actually cares about preserving ecosystems.
What kind of planet are we going to have? What can we do as teens to take action? We feel more powerful when we act together.
