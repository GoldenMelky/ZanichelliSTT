I’ve been thinking a lot about future careers recently and find the idea of becoming an engineer or some kind of scientist really attractive.
I love the thought of being able to create something innovative and useful.
Perhaps I could invent a new way of travelling – electric skateboards or flying bicycles – but maybe that’s been done already.
Or I could become a research chemist and discover a more efficient painkiller, or even a new formula to include in vaccinations against future pandemics.
There are so many new developments in the medical field all the time, it’s hard to think what invention or discovery might be next.
Careful observation, experiments and research has led in the past to the discovery of vital medicines like penicillin.
I can just picture Alexander Fleming when he realised he had discovered the possible solution to important health problems.
It’s challenging but exciting to see myself creating or discovering in a white coat in a laboratory.
