<i>What is hate speech?</i> Hate speech to me is when somebody is rude or uses slurs against a certain minority group or somebody who is, has a disability or, things like that.
Hate speech is people who are trying to make you feel bad online for sharing a view, which might be your opinion and somebody with a different opinion disagrees with you and is negative towards you because of it.
<i>Have you encountered hate speech online? </i> I think everyone’s encountered hate speech online.
It’s unavoidable.
I haven’t, no.
I know a couple of friends that have encountered hate speech but they didn’t really elaborate about it, they weren’t very happy about it.
<i>Why do people post hate speech online?</i> I feel that people post hate speech online because they have a sense of safety behind a screen.
As, I don’t think as many people would go up to someone and say those hurtful things to their face if it wasn’t behind a screen.
I think they think it makes them look cool or powerful, like, if they don’t like someone then they’ll say something about it, and they think it makes them strong or makes them good for getting rid of the bad people but actually it’s just being unkind.
<i>How do you feel when you see hate speech online?</i> It makes me feel sad, because, you know, we’re in the 21<sup>st</sup> century and equality should be happening but, even on social media, it’s not and people are very hateful towards each other and it’s not nice.
<i>How do you react when you see hate speech online?</i> When one of my friends had a hate speech or they knew someone who had a hate speech towards them and they contacted the police so that it could be stopped.
If one of my friends have had hate speech targeted at them online, I usually tell them to block them and report it and don’t worry about it because you don’t know the person.
<i>Do you think hate speech is similar to bullying?</i> Bullying feels personal, in the sense that it’s typically targeted at someone, specifically, repeatedly, daily.
Hate speech is whole groups of people or casual and off the cuff and just because you felt like it.
Definitely, yeah.
I think in some ways it’s worse than bullying because you don’t know who the person is.
It’s a complete stranger and they’re not always nice.
<i>Do you think girls are more commonly victims of hate speech?</i> Yes, I think so.
Because, despite the fact that everyone, there is a lot more equality these days, and the world is a lot more fair, a lot of people still have this prejudice that girls are weaker, that girls are less likely to stand up for themselves, that they need protecting and that they are easy targets.
