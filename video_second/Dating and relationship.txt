This is the story of Joanne and Stuart.
They met at a basketball match where Stuart was playing for the school team.
Joanne had a crush on Stuart and was over the moon when he asked her to go on a date.
They went out together for two months before they quarrelled and split up.
Joanne thought Stuart was cheating on her and they had a tremendous row! Joanne decided she couldn’t stay with someone she didn’t trust.
Stuart was really sad.
He went to see Joanne and took a bunch of flowers and some chocolates.
Then he said that she needed to get to know him better.
Joanne cried but then they made up and all was well.
All their friends were really happy because they considered them a match made in heaven!
