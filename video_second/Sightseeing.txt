Hi there everyone, let me tell you about our school trip to Edinburgh.
Yes, we went on a class coach trip to the capital of Scotland.
It certainly is a beautiful and stunning city.
We arrived in the morning and went round the castle, then we walked down the Royal Mile and saw the cathedral before visiting the historic art gallery.
We had lunch in a spacious square called Charlotte Square.
It didn’t have a fountain but there was a huge statue of Prince Albert.
In the afternoon we visited Holyrood Palace, where Mary Queen of Scots lived.
Then we looked around the National Monument on Calton Hill.
There’s also a tower there built in honour of Admiral Nelson.
After that we hit the shops! We had some free time and I bought tartan scarves for Mum and Dad and a model of the Loch Ness monster for Isaac – as a joke! We met up outside the Usher Hall, a big theatre and concert hall, and headed for home.
The coach dropped us off at school late in the evening.
It was a truly great day out.
