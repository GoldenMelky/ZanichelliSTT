Isaac and Mazie are in an art gallery.
Here they are in front of a portrait of Isaac’s favourite hero, Alexander the Great.
Now they’re looking at landscapes and seascapes.
Mazie thinks that if you focus on the painting, you can imagine yourself inside it.
Isaac prefers the sculptures.
He thinks they are more solid and he would like to touch them to feel the stone, but it’s forbidden.
Now they are in the modern section and Isaac is looking at some abstract painting.
He loves the use of colour and the shapes.
He even likes the still lifes when they are painted in an abstract way.
Mazie doesn’t agree.
She prefers the pictures of scenes with people, walking through the park or performing simple actions in their homes.
She’d like to be able to paint and be artistic.
She sometimes thinks she’d like to be an architect designing interesting buildings or a photographer taking unusual photographs.
While she’s dreaming about becoming famous, Isaac has decided it’s time for a coffee break and is looking for the gallery café.
