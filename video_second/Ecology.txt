Listening to the news has become quite depressing! One of the worrying issues that is often in the news is climate change.
We have all learnt the terminology.
Global warming is the gradual increase in temperature of the Earth’s atmosphere.
This is caused by pollution from the use of fossil fuels as sources of energy rather than renewable sources like solar energy from solar panels.
The greenhouse effect is where the extra warmth is trapped in our atmosphere causing environmental problems.
There is also deforestation taking away trees which absorb harmful carbon dioxide and give out life-saving oxygen.
We all need to be aware of the threat to biodiversity and protect endangered plants and animals.
We do try.
We all look carefully at waste disposal, recycling glass, paper and plastic waste.
We think carefully about the use of water and harmful emissions that might affect the quality of our air.
We try to reduce, recycle and save where we can, and damage, pollute and throw away as little as possible.
If we all become eco-warriors, who knows, the news might become positive!
