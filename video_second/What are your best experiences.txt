<i>When was your best birthday?</i> Three years ago.
I organised a sleepover with party games.
Everyone hung out in their pyjamas, ate snack food, and played board games.
I’ve got fond memories of birthday parties in my childhood.
Party food and games and watching cartoons for as long as our parents let us stay up.
<i>How did you find the courage </i> <i>to talk to the boy or girl you fancy?</i> Well, I think it was twenty seconds of insane courage! After that, we went out on a first date.
I thought: if he’s a kind person, he’ll at least stop to chat.
So, I went for it! <i>What was your best first date?</i> Last summer I went for a walk in the park with a friend from school.
It was dark and we sat in the middle of the grass, looking at the stars and talking.
It was really exciting.
We didn’t touch our phones at all.
We just ate pizza and talked.
<i>What about the first kiss?</i> One day after school, the girl I fancied walked me to the bus stop.
Just before I got on the bus, she looked into my eyes and then kissed me.
I was smiling all the way home.
We were at a concert and when my favourite song came on, he decided to make the first move.
It was super romantic! <i>How did you choose your secondary school?</i> The feeling I had when I visited it.
Going to secondary school changed my life.
I knew the most important factor was how the school made me feel.
I know I made the right choice because it feels like home.
I looked carefully at the courses on offer and the scholarships: I was aware that the school you choose is the place where you’ll be able to grow in all aspects of your life.
