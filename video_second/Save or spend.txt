Hey guys! It’s Yola and Rohaan here.
Today’s vlog is about money.
‘Money doesn’t buy happiness.’ To be honest, I find this idea impossible to justify.
Anyone who says a million pounds won’t make them happier must be a bit confused.
I personally think money can increase happiness, and not just because the extra money means that you can afford everything that you want.
I like the idea of the freedom money can give you.
It gives you more control over your life.
It gives you the freedom to retire early, the security to support yourself and your family, the thrill of spending on exciting experiences, and the option of lending to friends in need or donating to charities.
And lots more.
Save or spend? I’ve never been flat broke! Whenever I get some money from relatives for my birthday or at Christmas, I prefer to save for a rainy day and seldom take out the money because I don’t want to waste it on useless stuff.
Is it better to spend or save? It’s always exciting to receive money to spend on the trendiest hoodie or stuff like that, whether it’s the money that you have earned, pocket money or gifts from your relatives.
I keep two money boxes: one for spending that will give me the chance of buying something new, and one for saving for a rainy day.
A lot of people think that saving is not fun.
Do you know any ways to make it enjoyable? This is my idea.
Instead of spending all of your pocket money, you can start by saving £1 out of every £5 you have.
Easy, isn’t it? Bye!
