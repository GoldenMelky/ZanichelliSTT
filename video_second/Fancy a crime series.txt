Hi, there! Everyone loves a little binge TV watching.
I’ve just watched the latest episode of <i>One of Us Is Lying</i>, which is based on a cool mystery novel.
There’s this unfriendly guy, Simon, who has a real talent for finding out the secrets of his fellow students at Bayview High School.
He uses About That, a gossip app he created to spread unpleasant comments and rumours about his peers.
One day he enters detention with four other students: cheerleader Addy, top student Bronwyn, baseball player Cooper and Nate, who is very popular with girls… well, lots of girls my age fancy him… After drinking a glass of water, Simon dies because of a fatal allergic reaction.
The police interview the four students and find that each of them had a motive for murdering Simon.
Simon had found out that Addy had cheated on her boyfriend, that Nate had dealt drugs and Bronwyn had hacked her Chemistry teacher’s computer and stolen the exam answers.
Cooper was accused of using steroids.
Will any of them confess or will they get away with it? I’m not going to give any spoilers! There’s another series I’m really into, but you have to watch it with subtitles.
