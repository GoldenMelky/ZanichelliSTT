Hi everyone! Are you someone who likes keeping up to date with technology? To be honest, I am! I can be considered a techie! What’s a techie? It’s someone who has good technology skills and is enthusiastic about technology, especially computing.
Here are some signs that you might be a techie, too.
You can hardly spend a day without technology, and you are frequently scrolling up and down on your phone for posts, news, emails, texts and so on.
You want to know about everything tech related.
And you’ve got more gadgets than you probably need.
A smartwatch, a tablet, a laptop, your earbuds… you have all your technological devices to hand: a printer, a wireless keyboard, a Bluetooth speaker, a USB-c cable and a memory stick.
You prefer to spend money on new programmes or games rather than on clothes or food.
You spend your money on a good powerbank or a pair of super headphones.
Or maybe you are saving for a VR headset or a webcam.
You know your priorities! You are curious about new technologies, you read tech blogs, watch tech videos, listen to podcasts that reveal what high-tech devices are coming out.
You often use tech words in your chats.
OK, maybe your vocab isn’t filled with tech terms, but you understand when someone else says these words and you even get thrilled about it! I’m not saying you’re antisocial, but maybe you prefer to spend your free time with your electronic devices.
I get it! Maybe you have just bought your new gaming controller for your games console and can’t wait to use it! So, if you feel like you relate to any of these symptoms of being a techie, well, it’s all right: we’re techie-mates, we’re on the same page!
