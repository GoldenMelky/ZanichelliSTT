Do you want to buy a computer? Do you want a laptop, a tablet or a desktop computer? First of all you need the right equipment.
Perhaps a laptop is the most versatile.
It has a keyboard, screen, microphone and speakers all included.
You can use its touchpad to click or scroll up or down.
If you choose to use a mouse, you might need a mouse mat.
A laptop has a good battery, but you’ll also need a charger to charge it, with a cable and plug.
Make sure the computer has enough USB ports for the pen drives and also a connector for your headphones.
There are further extras to be bought, if you want, like a printer or maybe Bluetooth earbuds.
Some people also buy an extra monitor to give them a bigger screen.
You can also connect the device to your phone.
Make sure you have a USB-C cable.
It’s the same cable you use with your powerbank when your phone is running out of charge.
