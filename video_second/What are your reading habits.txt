<i>What is your favourite</i> <i>and least favourite book genre?</i> Fantasy novels are my favourite.
My least favourite are historical novels.
My favourite books are crime novels but I’m not really into romantic novels.
<i>What genre are you reading right now?</i> A sci-fi thriller.
I’m reading a self-help book.
<i>Can you name a novel that kept you up at night? </i> <i>Scorpia</i>, one of the best-selling Alex Rider series.
I read it from cover to cover in one day, I couldn’t put it down.
Whenever I read a crime novel, it keeps me up because the plot twists are so gripping, but then I’ve always got my nose in a book.
<i>Is there a book you just couldn’t finish?</i> <i>Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince</i>.
When it hit the shelves, I bought it straightaway, but I found it so disappointing that I just gave up.
Last year I had to read a biography for school, I couldn’t finish it because it was too boring.
<i>Do you ever read the first or last page before buying a book?</i> Sometimes I read the first page to get a feel of the book.
Well, just a few lines from the first few pages, but not too much because I hate spoilers! <i>Do you ever underline things</i> <i>or write notes in books?</i> Not usually, I did once in a book about football because I wanted to remember some stats.
Yes, I do.
I usually write notes in the chapters containing the crucial details of the story, especially when the plot is very complex.
<i>Is there a character in one of your books </i> <i>that you would like to meet?</i> Bilbo Baggins from The Hobbit.
I’d like to see what he really looks like with his hairy feet and visit his hobbit house.
Yes, last month I read a graphic novel and the heroine was so clever and funny that I would love to meet her.
