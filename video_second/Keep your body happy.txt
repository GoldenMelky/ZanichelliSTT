Hi guys! Do you want to try some daily moves to keep your body happy? Let’s start.
These are moves that you can easily work into your usual daily routine.
Stand on one leg while you brush your teeth.
Seven times per leg.
It improves your balance.
If you want to make it more challenging, close your eyes while balancing.
Just sit on the floor like this, with your legs crossed, while you watch your favourite TV series.
It will make your hips super flexible! Can you rise from a cross-legged position without placing a hand or knee on the floor? I’m gonna try… Keep your fingers crossed! I don’t wanna twist my ankle… Don’t worry if you can’t do it – it takes practice, but it’s worth working on.
Why? Because if you can get up without any support, it shows that your body is stable and efficient.
And this is the ideal squat! Look at this.
Bend your knees, make sure your bottom is just a few inches above the floor, point your toes forward, feet flat on the floor.
In some cultures, squatting is as common as sitting on a chair.
This is great for the knees, hips and back.
Remember arm circles in PE? They are super to keep shoulder and neck muscles fit.
Stand tall with your arms above your head.
Circle your arms in wide arcs out and down 10 times.
Repeat in the opposite direction.
Lastly, connect with the earth.
As you know, our feet are the body’s foundation and help us maintain balance.
