<i>Isaac and his mum are talking about money.</i> Hey Mum, have you got a minute? Sure, what’s the problem? It’s so messy dealing with banknotes and coins.
I’d like to have a bankcard.
Well, which one? There are different types of cards.
Can you explain the difference? OK. There’s a credit card, which you can use in shops or online and it takes money from your bank account at a later time, or a debit card, which you can use in the same way, but it takes money straight from your bank account.
My friend Joanne has a prepaid card.
What’s that? That’s actually a really good idea because you can put a limited amount of money on it and keep an eye on what you spend.
How do you put money on it? Do you need to do a bank transfer? No, you don’t.
You can simply use an app.
I think that’s the best.
Better than using cash and withdrawing money from a cash machine.
And you can put it on your phone and make mobile payments! What about if I go abroad? You can use your card or phone without having to get foreign currency.
It’s just what I need!
