Hi guys! Do you remember me? I’m Eirin.
I live in Clevedon, a seaside town on the west coast of England.
The Somerset countryside around is full of villages and pretty cottages, like the one I used to live in before I moved here ten years ago.
I love living in Clevedon and I can’t imagine living anywhere else.
What’s good about my town? To be honest, living in Clevedon suits me.
This may be quite a surprise because most young people like the hustle and bustle of a city, but I’m really into quieter places with more residential areas.
Here you can get to know your neighbours and have a sense of community.
We’ve got loads of facilities: hotels, restaurants, several playgrounds and a fire station, but no railway station yet.
On the downside, there is not much night life and sometimes it feels a bit cut off from the wider world.
Public transport is OK, but traffic can be an issue at weekends because people from Bristol and Bath take the motorway to drive out of town and find a quiet place.
My town hasn’t changed a lot in the last few years.
Some people are moving here for work and it’s a bit more crowded than it used to be.
But there are no big new office blocks in the centre and the suburbs haven’t grown.
You know, with the sea on one side, and the motorway on the other, there isn’t much space.
The seafront roads now have much better access for pedestrians and cyclists.
