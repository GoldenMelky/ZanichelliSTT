Travelling can be very stressful, especially if you are travelling abroad on a long-distance flight.
First you have to pack your suitcase.
Then you need to set off much earlier than your flight will actually take off.
You have to check in and board the plane.
Usually, you have a limited amount of hand luggage you can take on board with you.
Other luggage is called hold luggage and goes in the airplane's hold.
You will collect it at your destination when you arrive.
Another problem with this kind of travel is times.
The departure is seldom on time and the arrival is often late.
If you are very late, you sometimes miss the transfer to your next flight.
When this happens, you might have to have an unscheduled stopover.
That is when you have to spend a night in a nearby hotel between flights.
Whatever your destination, whether you are on a long-haul flight or have booked a long sea crossing, remember that the journey is the first part of your holiday or trip, so try and relax and enjoy it.
