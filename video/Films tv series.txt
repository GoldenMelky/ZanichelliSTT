Isaac and Mum want to watch a film together, but they can't decide which film to see.
Isaac likes action or adventure films with lots of action and unforgettable special effects.
Mum likes romantic comedies or musicals with a touching romantic story and a happy ending.
She also likes historical drama where she can imagine herself playing the part of the heroine.
Naturally, Isaac hates rom com, musicals and historical drama.
But he likes science-fiction films.
Mum suggests <i>E.T.,</i> but Isaac wasn't thinking of that kind of romantic science-fiction.
He was thinking of <i>Star Wars.</i> But his mum shakes her head.
Isaac suggests an iconic horror film or a blockbuster thriller.
Mum suggests a fantasy film or a cartoon, something by a famous director.
Isaac and his mum are fed up.
They were hoping to have a relaxing evening in front of a good film, but it doesn't look possible.
Then Isaac has a great idea.
He suggests a film called <i>Glass Onion</i> starring Daniel Craig.
It's a mystery thriller, but it's also a comedy drama with a terrific cast and complicated but entertaining plot.
Hurrah! Now they are both happy.
Enjoy the film.
