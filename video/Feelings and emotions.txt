I'm an emotional person and like to recognise my feelings.
For example, I feel proud of myself when I do well at school, but sorry about a bad mark in a test.
I love playing basketball and I feel delighted with my team when we win, but I get very cross with everyone when we lose.
I'm grateful when someone gives me a present to cheer me up, but get upset with my family if they forget my birthday.
I love being with people and am always excited about meeting new friends.
I suppose I'm a real optimist, always hopeful about the future.
I like being punctual and feel ashamed of sometimes being late for school because of sleeping in.
Are there times when I feel afraid of stuff? Maybe sometimes but not very often.
The only thing I am afraid of is… spiders!
