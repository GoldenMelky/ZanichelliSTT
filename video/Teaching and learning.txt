Let's think about school and all the different aspects that make up an efficient education.
First, we'll look at the learning process.
So, the students' role is to attend lessons, and you must try to be punctual and pay attention.
Students also need to study from their books and notes, and look up anything they don't understand.
Then they must revise it and go over it many times.
That is the way to make progress.
Remember that if you miss classes you need to catch up, which means you must do all the homework or schoolwork that you have missed.
If you always do your homework and study well, you will hand in your work to the teacher at the correct time and get a good mark when you take a test or exam.
The teacher, apart from explaining the information to you, is the person who will assess or evaluate your work and give you a grade or a mark.
If you get a positive mark, you will pass the exam or test, but if you get a negative mark, you will fail, which means you will have to go back and revise again and study harder.
