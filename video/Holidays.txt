Let's look at the advantages and disadvantages of different means of transport.
In cities the best means of transport is definitely the underground.
It's quicker than a bus or a tram and you don't need to find a car park.
It is not expensive, but can be crowded.
If you want to be independent in town, you can use a bike, maybe from a bike-sharing app, or an electric scooter or a motorbike.
If you do, be careful of any vans or lorries! Outside the city you can take a train which is a comfortable way to travel, or maybe a coach, which is usually slower but cheaper.
If you are going further, you can take a plane or if you are really rich, take a helicopter, which is the most expensive! To cross water, you need a ferry or perhaps you know someone with a boat or ship.
Of course, if you go on holiday, you also have to think of where to stay.
If you have a camper van, that's a cheap option, and also mobile.
Other cheap accommodation could be a hostel, a bed and breakfast, B&B, or a campsite if you have a tent.
If you want something more luxurious, you can stay in a hotel or resort.
Whatever kind of holiday or transport you choose, remember to relax and enjoy it!
