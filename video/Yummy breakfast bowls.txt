Hi guys! It's Eirin here and it's breakfast time! As you know, the traditional English breakfast is a national institution, but it's not really my cup of tea! Too much food! There are some… fried eggs, sausages, bacon, tomatoes, mushrooms and sometimes a slice of black pudding.
But how many English people actually have a full English breakfast nowadays? Well, except my granddad… A lot of working people and most youngsters just have cereal or a slice of toast with butter and jam, and a cup of tea or instant coffee.
Breakfast is definitely my favourite meal and I'm a great fan of yummy breakfast bowls.
Some are sweet and others are savoury.
So, today I'm showing you how to make a tasty go-to bowl! When I'm in a hurry, I make a delicious smoothie bowl in under five minutes! I take… frozen banana, a cup and a half of fresh strawberries, and a half a cup of coconut milk.
Then I throw the ingredients into a blender and mix until they're smooth.
Finally, I pour the mixture into a bowl and add different toppings, like a handful of almonds… blueberries… chocolate chips… and some banana slices, peanut butter or cereal.
Take your pick!
