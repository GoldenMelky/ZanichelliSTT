What career are you going to take up in the future? Let's look at some possibilities.
What about being a surgeon or a nurse working in a hospital or maybe a dentist looking after teeth? Those who like Maths and designing things can be future engineers.
Or a career as a fashion designer making trendy clothes or an architect creating beautiful buildings.
A more active job is a brave firefighter putting out fires or a personal trainer in a gym keeping people fit.
There are jobs in tourism like a tour guide or a flight attendant.
Those who like flying can become pilots.
If you are passionate about justice, you can become a lawyer and follow a legal profession.
A more practical career is a carpenter working with wood, a hairdresser cutting hair, or a vet working with animals.
Perhaps you are a future mechanic repairing cars or an electrician dealing with electrics.
Restaurants need imaginative chefs to cook innovative dishes and waiters to serve at table.
Offices need accountants to work with numbers, managers to run the company and secretaries to help them with the routine correspondence.
We also need scientists to research into new discoveries.
There are so many fascinating jobs.
The difficulty is choosing which one to go for!
