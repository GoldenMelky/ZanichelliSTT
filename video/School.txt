Here's a typical secondary school with students from 11 to 18.
There are about 1,000 students at this school.
Let's look at the plan.
This is the entrance where all the students' lockers are.
In the entrance there is also the headteacher's room and the burser's office.
The burser is in charge of the money.
Next there is the assembly hall where school meetings and assemblies take place as well as plays and concerts.
Behind the hall is the school canteen where lunch happens.
The gym is on this floor too for PE lessons, and the library, with its librarian.
There's also a small room that the school counsellor uses to chat to students about their problems.
Upstairs there are lots of classrooms for subjects like English, History, Geography, Psychology, Maths and Economics.
There's a Drama room, Music room and Art room.
There's a language lab and a science lab with technicians.
Over there is the staff room for teachers, and a sixth form common room for students in the last two years of school.
Many of them are prefects who have to help keep discipline and generally give the teachers a hand.
This little room is for the caretaker: he keeps all the cleaning stuff in here.
