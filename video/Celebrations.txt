What do you celebrate in your family? In my family we all love celebrating and last year was a particularly good year.
First of all, we went to Venice for the Carnival.
We dressed up in costumes and watched a fantastic parade.
While we were there, we also celebrated Valentine's Day on 14th February.
My dad gave my mum a card with a heart on it and a red rose.
It was very romantic! Then at Easter we stayed at home and looked for chocolate eggs in the garden.
I found ten! In May my granny baked a wonderful cake for my birthday and decorated it with chocolate chips and candles.
After the summer we had Halloween.
My sister and I dressed up as ghosts and made a pumpkin lantern.
In November we celebrated Thanksgiving with our American friends.
They prepared this enormous meal with turkey, sweet potatoes and pumpkin pie.
After that, we all looked forward to Christmas.
We decorated our Christmas tree.
Then on Christmas morning, we opened all our presents and sang carols.
The final celebration of the year was in the mountains.
We set off fireworks at midnight and then we all skied down to the village with torches.
Wonderful fun! Yes, it was New Year's Eve!
