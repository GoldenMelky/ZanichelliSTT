<i>Here is Sylvia explaining about the world of work.</i> Well, the first thing to say is that everyone hopes to find an interesting job, that is a position where you work and get paid.
Different kinds of jobs have different wages or salaries.
A wage is normally for manual work with a weekly payment, whereas a salary is for other types of job which have a monthly payment.
In job advertisements there is usually a weekly wage given or an annual salary depending on the job.
A well-paid job will have a good salary, while a low-paid job will have a small salary or wage.
An employer is the person who gives you the work.
They are also known as the boss.
He or she employs you and you are their employee.
The job could be a part-time job with reduced hours, or a full-time job with eight hours a day, also called a nine-to-five job, because the usual hours are 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.
Your first job could be a temporary job for a short time or maybe a summer job during your school holidays.
Have you ever tried applying for a summer job? You must apply usually in writing in an email with a CV attached and hope to have a job interview… and a job offer!
